# 52 — Framework `0.2.0-beta.4`

## What changed

| Dependency | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `@igrp/framework-next` | `0.2.0-beta.3` | `0.2.0-beta.4` |
| `@igrp/framework-next-auth` | `0.2.0-beta.2` | `0.2.0-beta.3` |
| `@igrp/framework-next-types` | `0.2.0-beta.2` | `0.2.0-beta.3` |
| `@igrp/framework-next-ui` | `0.2.0-beta.3` | `0.2.0-beta.4` |

`@igrp/igrp-framework-react-design-system` stays at `0.2.0-beta.3`.
No template source files change.

## Why

- `next-auth`: an app **without** `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH` now names its session
  cookie `next-auth.session-token~` instead of the stock name. The stock name
  is a prefix of every basePath app's cookie, and NextAuth reads session cookies
  by prefix, so a root-path app sharing a host with basePath apps glued their
  cookies onto its own, failed with `Invalid Compact JWE` (`JWT_SESSION_ERROR`),
  and then expired all of them. Switching between any two apps on the domain
  sent users back to `/login`.
- `next`: access-token recovery in server actions reads the new root-path name.
  `IGRPRootLayout` also accepts an optional `lang` prop for `<html lang>`
  (default `"pt"`, unchanged output).

`next-types` and `next-ui` only pick up the versions above.

## ⚠️ Deploy note

- Users of a root-path app are signed out once. Their old stock-named cookie
  stays until it expires; `igrpDeleteAuthCookies()` from `@igrp/framework-next`
  removes it.
- **Upgrade every app that shares the host.** One app left on an older version
  keeps the stock name and keeps breaking the others.
