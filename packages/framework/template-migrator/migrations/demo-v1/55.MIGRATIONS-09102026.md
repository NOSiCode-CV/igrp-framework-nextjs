No environment changes.

## Why

- **Stale auth cookies.** From `@igrp/framework-next-auth` `0.2.0-beta.4` the
  auth cookies are scoped to the app's basePath under a new name. The names the
  app used to write at `Path=/` are no longer read, but the browser keeps
  sending them to every path on the host, the IdP included, until they expire.
  The middleware now expires them. `staleAuthCookies` derives the names from
  this app's basePath only, so another co-hosted app's cookies are never
  touched. Once the old cookies are gone this does nothing, and it never does
  anything for a root-path app.
- **Framework `0.2.0-beta.6`.** Brings the next-auth token-lifecycle and
  provider-resolution hardening, and the `IGRPAuthCarousel` image-loading fix
  (see each package's CHANGELOG).

## ⚠️ If you customised these files

- `src/middleware.ts` is replaced. If you added matchers or redirects, re-apply
  them on top of the new file, and keep the `expireStaleAuthCookies(...)` wrap
  around the response.
- Anything importing `@/components/header/app-search` must import
  `@/components/app-search` instead.
