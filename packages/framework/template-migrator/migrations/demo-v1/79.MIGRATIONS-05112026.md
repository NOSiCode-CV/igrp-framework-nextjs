# 79 — framework-next 0.2.0-beta.18

## What changed

| Dependency | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `@igrp/framework-next` | `0.2.0-beta.17` | `0.2.0-beta.18` |
| `@igrp/framework-next-ui` | `0.2.0-beta.15` | `0.2.0-beta.16` |
| `@igrp/igrp-framework-react-design-system` | `0.2.0-beta.11` | `0.2.0-beta.12` |

## Why

- `IGRPDataTableFilterFaceted` scrolls when it has many options. Before, the popover grew past the bottom of the screen and the extra options could not be reached.
- `IGRPDataTableClientFilter` takes a `className` for its wrapper.

`framework-next` and `framework-next-ui` only move to pick up the new design system.

## Behaviour changes to check

- **`IGRPDataTableFilterSelect`** caps its list at `max-h-72` (about 288px) and scrolls, instead of filling the space to the screen edge. A `max-h-*` class passed through `className` still overrides it.

## After migrating

Run `pnpm install`.
