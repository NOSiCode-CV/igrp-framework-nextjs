# 82 — framework-next 0.2.0-beta.20

## What changed

| Dependency | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `@igrp/framework-next` | `0.2.0-beta.19` | `0.2.0-beta.20` |
| `@igrp/framework-next-ui` | `0.2.0-beta.17` | `0.2.0-beta.18` |
| `@igrp/igrp-framework-react-design-system` | `0.2.0-beta.13` | `0.2.0-beta.14` |

## Why

- Rich-text links can no longer take `class`, `rel` or `target` from stored or pasted HTML. Before, stored content could lay an invisible full-page link over the app or bring back reverse tabnabbing.
- `IGRPRichTextView` no longer freezes the tab on a stored body made of many unclosed `<` characters.

`framework-next` and `framework-next-ui` only move to pick up the new design system.

## Behaviour changes to check

- **Rich-text links** from stored or pasted HTML always render with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`, and any `class` is dropped. Links made with the editor are unchanged. Content that relied on `target="_self"` or a custom class on a link now gets the defaults.

## After migrating

Run `pnpm install`.
